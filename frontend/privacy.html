Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect usage data including resume text, interview answers, and chat history with our AI Mentor.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to provide and improve the CyberVerse AI service. We use your inputs to generate personalized feedback, mock interviews, and daily study plans. We do not sell your personal information or resume data to advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

We share data only with essential service providers to operate the app:

  • Anthropic: To process AI prompts and generate feedback.
  • Razorpay: To process payments securely (we do not store your card details).
  • Resend: To send transactional emails like password resets and welcome messages.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing support@grcwithgaurav.com.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure password hashing, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
