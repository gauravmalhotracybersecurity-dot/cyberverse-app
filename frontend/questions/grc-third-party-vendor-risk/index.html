GRC interview question

How do you assess third-party and vendor risk?

What interviewers are really testing

  • Tiering by data access and criticality
  • Evidence over questionnaires where possible
  • Contractual and continuous-monitoring view

A strong answer framework

  1. Tier vendors by data access and business criticality
  2. Send proportionate assessments (light for low tier)
  3. Review evidence: SOC 2, ISO certs, pen test summaries
  4. Check clauses: breach notification, audit rights, sub-processors
  5. Set review cadence and monitor vendor incidents

Follow-ups you should be ready for

  • What if a critical vendor has no SOC 2?
  • How do you handle fourth parties?
Reading answers is not the same as defending them.
